Meet Bento: My cat

Bento's Daily Routine

Bento loves two things: sleeping and eating. He spends most of his day resting around the house and waking up whenever it's time for food. Although he seems lazy during the day, his personality completely changes at night.

Bento's Favorite Activities

At night, Bento becomes extremely playful. His favorite game is chasing me around the house. He also loves jumping from one piece of furniture to another, treating the entire house like his personal playground. Watching him run around is one of my favorite things about having him as a cat.
